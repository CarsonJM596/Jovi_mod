# Crush — Stand Concept

## Overview

* **Stand Name:** Crush
* **Namesake:** Bon Jovi — *Crush* (2000)
* **Stand User:** Jovi Joestar
* **Status:** Concept Development

## 1. Inspiration and Identity

### Namesake

Crush draws inspiration from Bon Jovi's Crush and the many other songs that explore the different meanings of a crush, attraction, and emotional attachment. However, its identity extends beyond its musical namesakes.

Crush is a Stand that manipulates and grows stronger from the emotions of both its user and those around him. It can induce or amplify emotional states, making its targets genuinely believe that what they feel is their own authentic emotion.

However, Crush is not quite mind control. It can influence how someone feels, but it cannot dictate how they act on those feelings. Someone might feel fear without choosing to run, affection without choosing to trust, or anger without choosing to attack. The emotion is real, but the response to it remains their own.

This distinction is what makes Crush dangerous. By manipulating the emotional state of an opponent, Jovi can disrupt their judgment, weaken their resolve, and create openings that would otherwise be impossible to exploit.

Jovi doesn't need to be the strongest person in the room. He needs to understand what his opponent is feeling, anticipate how they might respond, and use that knowledge to turn a difficult fight in his favor.

### Core Theme

Jovi's entire existence has been a mystery to him. He knows nothing about his parents, has no understanding of his bloodline, and is completely unaware of the birthmark on his shoulder that connects him to a legacy far greater than he could imagine.

Rather than allow the weight of these unanswered questions to affect the people around him, Jovi has learned to keep his emotions in check—or at least, that's what he believes he's doing. He hides his uncertainty behind charm, humor, and a seemingly carefree attitude, convincing himself that as long as he doesn't burden others with his problems, everything will be fine.

This is where Crush is born.

Crush embodies Jovi's desire to control emotions: his own, as well as those of the people around him. What begins as an attempt to keep his feelings contained manifests as a power capable of influencing how others feel, making those emotions seem entirely genuine to the people experiencing them.

Yet there is an irony to this ability. Jovi believes that controlling emotions means preventing them from causing harm, but emotions cannot simply be managed away. They influence decisions, shape relationships, and persist even when someone tries to hide them.

Crush represents that contradiction. Emotions can be suppressed, redirected, or manipulated, but they cannot be separated from the people who experience them. And for someone who has spent his life hiding his own uncertainty, learning to understand those emotions may prove just as important as learning to control them.

## 2. Appearance and Personality

### Appearance

Crush is a strong, tall powerful looking stand, since it's destructive power like a lot of Joestar's is almost unmatched. The stand color reflects closely to the human heart to represent emotion and will be a close representation to a tall human male. His build will be very similar to Jovi's build as to represent the character, just a bit taller and bigger.

### Presence

Crush will manifest at the start every battle according to his Jovi's special relic, once the stand is defeated Jovi will also be defeated. The stand will reflect Jovi's HP bar not Jovi himself, as far as how the stand will interact with Slay the Spire 2, you will see him at the start of every battle and in shops

### Relationship with Jovi

Crush is supposed to represent Jovi's deep anger of never being able to know his father or his mother. The relationship between him and his stand is great because the stand understands and can express his pain better than himself. There will be no problems between the two other than the fact that Jovi hardly knows it's serious potential due to the fact that he literally just summoned his stand not long after getting stuck in here.

## 3. Fundamental Ability

### Core Power

Crush's power is simply to control emotion which in Jojo's is substantial. Since a stand is a manifestation of a soul and a soul consists of memory, intellect, and emotion he can control a critical part of a stand. Typically this would reflect on the destructive power or a stands speed and even sometimes it's precision. As emotion can create hesitation in the soul giving Jovi the upper hand if even for a brief moment. 

### Applications

In Slay the Spire 2 this can cause enemies to have serious status effect debuffs such as weak, or vulnerable exposing themselves to Jovi. He can also apply strength or heal himself or allies that he may run across as he affects them and their emotions. Jovi primarily will use Slay the Spire 2's in game status effects to buff himself or debuff the enemy.

### Limitations

The serious limitation of this ability is that every soul is different and responds differently to emotion which causes his precision to tank sometimes as Jovi can intend them to act one way but act completely differently than expected. Also some people aren't nearly as a effected by emotions than others so in some cases it seriously is a lot less powerful than most stands. Although in Slay the Spire 2 we are dealing with monsters, which have souls but relatively lower intellect and subject to falling for simple emotions (fear, love, anger) which is where Jovi thrives, so he will not experience such an issue here.

### Weaknesses and Counters

Jovi's stand not only is inaccurate a lot of the time but is also limited to very serious range only going up to about 3 meters. The further you get away from Jovi, the less intense the emotional connection you feel to the feeling he is inflicting on you. This causes very serious limitations to his ability as if you are smart, you will just run away if you start to feel off. Again this limitation has nothing to do with Slay the Spire however because enemies are constrained to a room.

## 5. Slay the Spire 2 Integration

### Starting Special Relic

At the beginning of combat, Jovi summons Crush through a distinctive posing animation. Crush remains manifested throughout the encounter and represents Jovi's fighting presence.

Crush does not have a separate HP pool. Its health is represented by Jovi's HP, and damage dealt to Jovi is visually represented as damage to Crush. If Jovi is defeated, Crush disappears with him.

The exact handling of buffs, debuffs, healing, and other combat effects will be determined during implementation to ensure compatibility with Slay the Spire 2's combat systems.

Crush will also appear alongside Jovi outside combat in designated scenes, such as shops, where appropriate.

### Unique Interactions

As of right now there are no necessarily unique reactions, this is subject to change though as we start to develop cards and push limitations.

### Balance Considerations

We want Jovi to primarily play around the statuses within Slay the Spire. He can do high damage but at serious costs and multiple turn setups that cost the player valuable time and energy within the game. We want to make him strong enough to make him feel like a powerful stand but not so strong that every monster within Slay the Spire 2 is a cake walk. We want him to be a strong character that is within the realm of balance for Slay the Spire 2.
